## Executive Summary

AtliQ Motors holds a 25% EV/hybrid market share in North America but maintains under 2% share in India. This study analyzed **57.2 million automotive vehicle sales** and **2.07 million EV registrations** across 35 Indian states and union territories between April 2021 and March 2024 (FY2022–FY2024) to guide AtliQ's market entry strategy.

### Headline Findings:
1. **Exponential EV Market Growth:** EV sales expanded at a **93.91% CAGR** from 271,150 units in FY2022 to 1,019,593 units in FY2024, far outpacing the overall automotive industry CAGR of 13.56%. Overall EV penetration increased from 1.65% in FY22 to 4.81% in FY24 (portfolio average: 3.61%).
2. **Category Asymmetry:** 2-Wheelers represent **92.6% of EV sales volume** (1.91M units), driven by mass affordability and FAME-II subsidies. However, 4-Wheeler EVs represent **58.3% of total industry revenue** (₹13,035 Cr in FY24) and experienced an even faster revenue growth rate of **+367.79%** over the two-year period.
3. **Geographic Concentration:** Over 52% of national EV registrations and the top 4 highest penetration rates are concentrated in the Southern/Western economic corridor: **Goa (13.75%), Kerala (11.59%), Karnataka (10.18%), and Maharashtra (8.60%)**.
4. **Competitive Moats:** Tata Motors commands over **68% of the 4W EV market**, having delivered 88,935 units over the study period. Incumbent success has been driven by repurposing proven ICE vehicle platforms into accessible mid-market electric crossovers (Nexon EV, Tiago EV).

## Strategic Market Entry Roadmap for AtliQ Motors

```
[Phase 1: Entry & Localization]  ──►  [Phase 2: Regional Rollout]  ──►  [Phase 3: Scale & Ecosystem]
* Build plant in Tamil Nadu          * Launch in South Corridor        * Battery Subscription (BaaS)
* Crossover SUV (₹18L–₹24L)            (KA, KL, GA, TN, MH)             * Intercity DC fast chargers
* Brand Ambassador campaign          * Establish 25 Experience Hubs     * Domestic vendor integration
```

### Recommendation 1: Launch Vehicle Segment & Price Positioning
- **The Pitfall to Avoid:** Entering with ultra-luxury CBU/import electric sedans (>₹60 Lakhs) where total addressable market volume is under 3,000 units annually across India.
- **The Recommended Product:** A compact-to-midsize **electric crossover / SUV priced between ₹18,00,000 and ₹25,00,000**, with a certified 60 kWh battery delivering a real-world range of 400+ km.
- **Value Proposition:** Compete directly against Tata Curvv EV, Tata Nexon EV Long Range, and MG ZS EV by offering AtliQ's superior US-engineered battery thermal management, high software integration (ADAS Level 2), and active cabin cooling designed for Indian extreme heat.

### Recommendation 2: Geographic Phasing — "South Corridor First"
- **Target Launch Markets:** Bengaluru (Karnataka), Kochi/Thiruvananthapuram (Kerala), Panaji (Goa), Chennai (Tamil Nadu), and Mumbai/Pune (Maharashtra).
- **Justification:**
  - High consumer purchasing power and early-adopter tech culture.
  - Established fast-charging infrastructure (over 55% of national chargers).
  - 100% state road-tax exemptions in Goa, Kerala, and Karnataka.
- **Northern Expansion:** Delay Tier-2 North/Central expansion until public fast-charging coverage achieves sufficient density along primary interstate national highways.

### Recommendation 3: Manufacturing Base in Tamil Nadu
- **Location:** Hosur / Sriperumbudur auto industrial corridor in **Tamil Nadu**.
- **Strategic Advantages:**
  - Plugs into existing EV ecosystem (Ola, Ather, TVS, Hyundai EV).
  - Access to 3 major commercial deep-water ports (Chennai, Ennore, Tuticorin) for component imports and future export to Southeast Asia.
  - Proximity to Karnataka (10.18% penetration) and Kerala (11.59% penetration), slashing finished-vehicle logistics costs.

### Recommendation 4: Brand Ambassadorship & Cultural Trust
- **Selected Figure:** **Neeraj Chopra** (Olympic Gold Medalist).
- **Brand Synergy:** Symbolizes precision, youthful aspiration, peak athletic performance, and global excellence. Effectively counters any perception of foreign disinterest and fosters intense domestic pride.
